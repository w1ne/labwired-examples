OLED CARRIER REV A — ELECTRONICS ASSEMBLY

The native project is oled-carrier.kicad_pro. Open it with KiCad 10.
This package contains a routed two-layer carrier, not a placed schematic export.
The Seeed XIAO and Adafruit display are purchased modules. Their internal PCBs
are not manufactured from the carrier Gerbers.

1. Fabricate the carrier using manufacturing/fabrication-settings.json and the
   Gerber/Excellon files. The four 2.9 mm mounting holes are non-plated.
2. Populate R1/R2 and J1 on the top side. Use the J1 pin-1 mark. Its cable faces
   toward the display (+X in hardware-interface.json).
3. Solder the two HSS-107-G-2 sockets at J2/J3. Use the module headers as an
   alignment fixture only while unpowered; keep all fourteen contacts parallel.
4. Solder the two BHS-107-G-A round-pin headers into the XIAO from its underside.
   The 0.46 mm tails fit the module's 0.85 mm native holes. The source-derived
   mated board-to-board gap is 9.77 mm. Do not replace these headers with 0.635 mm
   square posts: their 0.898 mm diagonal exceeds the native hole diameter.
5. Insert the XIAO with its USB-C connector facing the carrier left edge. J2 pin 1
   mates D0; J2 pins 3/4 mate D2/GPIO4 and D3/GPIO5. J3 pins 5/6 mate 3V3/GND.
   J3 pin 7 (VBUS) is intentionally not connected. Do not connect a battery.
6. Use the Adafruit 326 STEMMA QT revision. Close SJ3/ADDR to connect SA0 to GND
   for 0x3C. Keep the factory I2C-mode bridges SJ1/SJ2. Do not populate JP2.
   Connect either QT port to J1 using the Adafruit 4210 100 mm cable.
7. Keep the supplied RF antenna separate. This firmware does not enable Wi-Fi or
   Bluetooth. Native USB provides power, programming and debug. BOOT/RESET are
   accessible with the lid removed. The external antenna must be installed before
   using firmware that enables RF.

Unpowered checks before USB connection:
- Inspect J1 and both socket rows for solder bridges.
- Check that 3V3 and GND are not shorted. Check J1 pin order against the schematic.
- Check continuity: J2.3 -> J1.3; J2.4 -> J1.4; J3.5 -> J1.2; J3.6 -> J1.1.
- Check the mated header height, USB plug clearance, lid cable loop and screw
  clearance. Do not load the OLED glass with a screw or the lid.

Initial powered checks:
- Confirm a 3.3 V rail at TP1 relative to TP2 with the display connected.
- Confirm the I2C scan finds 0x3C on GPIO4/GPIO5 at 100 kHz.
- Run the package firmware and verify visible display updates and USB programming.
- Follow the top-level Build/Run/Assemble instructions for firmware and enclosure.

TP1=3V3, TP2=GND, TP3=SDA, TP4=SCL. These are bare copper pads, not BOM parts.
The display's 10 kohm external pull-ups parallel the carrier's 4.7 kohm resistors
(3.20 kohm nominal). Nominal LOW sink current is about 1.03 mA at 3.3 V, assuming a zero-volt LOW.

Validation scope: KiCad ERC, native routed DRC and schematic parity pass for the
committed sources. No prototype, electrical measurement, USB cable fit test,
thermal test or physical assembly test has been recorded. The native v1.3 XIAO
thickness field is 1.6 mm; its older official STEP uses 1.0 mm. The enclosure uses
the thicker board and conservative USB envelope. Confirm the received module.
