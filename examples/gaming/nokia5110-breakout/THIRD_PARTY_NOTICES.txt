Nokia 5110 Breakout carrier revision A

LabWired-authored carrier and wrapper source use the repository MIT license.
Manufacturer source files retain their original terms and attribution.

STMicroelectronics MB1136C native reference design:
The unmodified mb1136_bdp.zip is supplied by STMicroelectronics at
https://www.st.com/resource/en/board_manufacturing_specification/mb1136_bdp.zip
See references/st/provenance.json and open-platform-license-agreement.pdf.
The derived KiCad file uses the original Mechanical 1 finished contour, removes
one zero-length contour edge and corrects one 0.030 mm endpoint overlap so STEP
export can close the board perimeter. It is a purchased-module fit reference.
The original board is not relabelled as LabWired hardware or licensed under MIT.

KiCad symbol and footprint derivatives:
KiCad library contributors, CC-BY-SA-4.0 with the KiCad design exception.
See electronics/licenses/kicad-library-license.txt and electronics/provenance.json.

Joy-IT, Adafruit, Samtec, TDK, Yageo and ST names identify the selected products.
Their specification links do not imply endorsement. Native vendor CAD and
drawing-derived fit envelopes are identified separately in the mechanical package.

Rust firmware dependencies:
Cargo.lock pins cortex-m 0.7.7, cortex-m-rt 0.7.5 and panic-halt 0.2.0 plus their transitive
dependencies. Each dependency retains its upstream copyright and license. Cargo
fetches the corresponding source and license files for relinking. The package
firmware source is inherited from LabWired core under its MIT license.

Accu, Würth Elektronik and Panduit names identify purchased mechanical parts.
Their links in bom.csv are dimensional/source references, not endorsements.
The modeled hardware bodies are drawing-derived or declared fit envelopes.
